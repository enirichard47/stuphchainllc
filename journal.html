Payments July 2026

Inside Dogemeatpay: Getting Creators Paid Faster

Coming Soon

If you've ever sold music, art, or a service to someone in another country, you already know the problem. The money doesn't show up for days. A chunk of it disappears into fees before it even reaches you. And if you don't have the kind of banking history a traditional bank recognizes, you might not be able to accept the payment at all.

This isn't a talent problem. It's an infrastructure problem — and it's the exact gap Dogemeatpay, founded and developed directly by Stuph Chain LLC, was built to close.

Dogemeatpay is a payments platform built on fast, low-cost blockchain technology. Instead of routing a payment through a chain of banks and intermediaries — each one taking a cut and adding a delay — it lets money move directly and quickly, often in minutes rather than days. For a freelancer waiting on an invoice, or a small label waiting on streaming royalties, that difference isn't abstract. It's the difference between paying rent on time or not.

The platform is being built for the people traditional finance tends to overlook: independent artists, freelancers, and small businesses across Africa who earn real income but don't fit neatly into a legacy bank's paperwork. Dogemeatpay lets them accept payments from anywhere in the world, build a real financial track record over time, and hold onto more of what they earn instead of losing it to fees.

It also gives creators a way to turn their work into something they can actually manage — not just a payment received and spent, but a digital asset they own, can track, and can build on.

Dogemeatpay is currently in development. We're taking the "build it properly first" approach rather than rushing to launch — but if you want to be part of the early access group when it opens, get in touch and we'll keep you posted.

Apparel & Identity September 2026

Inside Locomotive Store: Identity You Can Wear

Launching Soon

Locomotive Store is the apparel and identity arm of Stuph Chain LLC. It was built on a simple idea: what you wear and how you present yourself should say something about who you are and where you're going.

The store brings together two things. The first is identity engineering: helping creators, artists, and brands shape a clear, consistent look and feel. The second is a signature clothing line designed to carry that same energy into everyday wear.

Every piece is made with intention, with clean design, quality materials, and a style that fits the Stuph Chain mindset that discipline is currency.

The signature collection is launching soon. More drops and updates will be shared here as the line grows.
