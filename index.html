FOUNDER PROFILE

Ademola Babajide
Ogegbo

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Stuph Chain LLC
Also known as Dogeson / Locomotive

Before building Stuph Chain, Ademola Babajide Ogegbo spent two years as an electrical engineer in the Signal & Telecom department at the Nigeria Railway Corporation. He studied Electrical/Electronics Engineering at Yaba College of Technology (YABATECH), then stepped away to establish Stuph Chain LLC as the Delaware parent entity in 2025. In music, tech, and creative circles, his steady, driving momentum earned him the moniker "Locomotive."

Under Stuph Chain LLC sit two companies: Stuph Chain Flys, handling travel and logistics, and Locomotive Store, focusing on identity engineering and a signature clothing line launching soon.

Ademola also founded Dogemeatpay, Stuph Chain LLC's digital payment platform, built to make transactions simple and borderless for creators, merchants, and everyday users.

Beyond logistics and commerce engines, Ademola is active in combat sports and the bodyXsol Web3 Academy — all built around one core mission: helping creators and entrepreneurs build, own, and scale real global businesses. His guiding principle says it best: discipline is currency.
