PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01

What we collect

Stuph Chain LLC collects the fields a transaction needs: name, email, delivery address, order details, and the payment reference returned by the processor. Card numbers are entered on the processor’s page. The company does not store full card numbers.

02

How we use it

We use this information to complete an order, prevent fraud, and answer a support request. We do not sell personal information. A payment partner, a delivery partner, or a host receives only the fields required to do that job.

03

Your choices

An account email can be corrected or deleted by writing to hello@dogemeatpay.info, except where a record must be kept for tax or a dispute. The site may set a session cookie so a checkout can finish.
