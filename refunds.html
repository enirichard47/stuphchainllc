REFUNDS

Refund Policy

How to ask for a refund, what qualifies, and how it is paid.

01

How to request a refund

Write to hello@dogemeatpay.info within 14 days of delivery. Include the order number.

02

What is refunded

A physical item that arrives damaged or not as described is refunded or replaced after a photo. A digital item that was not delivered is refunded. A digital item already delivered, or a blockchain payment already confirmed, is not refunded except where we failed to deliver.

03

How refunds are paid

Approved card refunds go back to the original card. We do not send a refund to a different name.
